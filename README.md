# TRADING-VIEW-RED
### The Open-Source Trading Intelligence Platform

**See the Market. Decode the Data. Trade Without Limits.**

## Introduction

TRADING-VIEW-RED is a next-generation, open-source financial charting and trading intelligence platform designed for traders, investors, developers, and financial enthusiasts who demand professional-grade market analysis without expensive subscription restrictions.

Built around the philosophy of open-source freedom, TRADING-VIEW-RED aims to bring advanced trading capabilities into one powerful, unified workspace. From professional charting and technical indicators to market scanners, strategy backtesting, options analysis, and paper trading, the platform is designed to provide everything traders need to understand financial markets and develop their own trading strategies.

Traditional trading platforms often place advanced features behind expensive premium subscriptions. TRADING-VIEW-RED takes a different approach by making advanced analytical capabilities accessible through an open and customizable ecosystem.

Whether you are an intraday trader analyzing NIFTY 50, a swing trader studying price action, a cryptocurrency enthusiast monitoring global markets, or a developer building algorithmic trading systems, TRADING-VIEW-RED provides a flexible environment for your workflow.

Our mission is simple: make powerful trading technology accessible, transparent, customizable, and community-driven.

## 1. Advanced Interactive Charting

TRADING-VIEW-RED provides a professional charting environment designed for detailed technical analysis.

Users can visualize financial instruments through multiple chart types, including candlestick, hollow candlestick, Heikin-Ashi, line, area, and bar charts.

The charting workspace supports multiple timeframes, from seconds and minutes to hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly intervals, depending on available market data.

Users can create multiple chart layouts, compare different instruments, customize chart appearances, and save their preferred configurations.

The drawing toolkit includes:

- Horizontal and vertical lines
- Trendlines and rays
- Fibonacci retracement and extensions
- Fibonacci channels
- Rectangle and price zones
- Support and resistance levels
- Supply and demand zones
- Parallel channels
- Long and short position measurement tools
- Risk/reward analysis tools

Every drawing should support customization, including colors, line styles, labels, and visibility settings.

## 2. Complete Technical Indicators Library

The platform aims to provide a comprehensive technical analysis library without restricting essential indicators behind premium subscriptions.

Users can apply multiple indicators to charts, customize their parameters, and combine them to develop their own trading strategies.

Supported indicators include:

- Simple Moving Average (SMA)
- Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
- Weighted Moving Average (WMA)
- Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)
- Relative Strength Index (RSI)
- Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)
- Bollinger Bands
- Average True Range (ATR)
- Average Directional Index (ADX)
- Stochastic Oscillator
- Supertrend
- Ichimoku Cloud
- Parabolic SAR
- On-Balance Volume (OBV)
- Money Flow Index (MFI)
- Commodity Channel Index (CCI)

Users can save indicator templates, create combinations, and extend the platform with custom indicators.

## 3. Multi-Market Intelligence

TRADING-VIEW-RED is designed to bring different financial markets into one centralized dashboard.

Supported market categories can include:

- Indian equities
- NSE and BSE stocks
- NIFTY 50 and BANK NIFTY
- Futures and options
- Global equities
- Forex markets
- Gold and silver
- Commodities
- Cryptocurrency
- Exchange-traded funds
- Global market indices

Users can monitor prices, percentage changes, daily highs and lows, opening prices, trading volumes, and other available market information.

A centralized search system allows users to find instruments and add them to customizable watchlists.

Real-time data availability depends on supported market-data providers, broker integrations, and exchange licensing.

## 4. Smart Watchlists and Market Scanner

TRADING-VIEW-RED includes advanced watchlist management and market-scanning capabilities.

Users can create multiple watchlists for intraday trading, swing trading, long-term investing, derivatives, and cryptocurrency.

The market scanner helps identify potential trading opportunities using configurable technical conditions.

Examples include:

- RSI below 30 or above 70.
- EMA 9 crossing above EMA 21.
- Price breaking previous resistance.
- Volume exceeding its average.
- Stocks approaching their 52-week highs.
- Price crossing VWAP.
- Bullish or bearish Supertrend changes.

Users can save scanning conditions and reuse them across different market sessions.

## 5. Strategy Builder and Backtesting

The platform provides an environment for creating, testing, and evaluating trading strategies.

Users can combine technical indicators, price-action conditions, volume filters, and risk-management rules to build custom strategies.

For example, a strategy can generate a potential entry when EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21, RSI confirms bullish momentum, and trading volume exceeds its average.

The historical backtesting engine evaluates these strategies using available historical market data.

Performance reports can include:

- Total trades
- Winning and losing trades
- Win rate
- Net profit and loss
- Maximum drawdown
- Profit factor
- Average risk/reward
- Equity curve
- Historical strategy performance

Brokerage, transaction costs, taxes, and slippage should be configurable to improve the accuracy of simulations.

## 6. Paper Trading and Virtual Portfolio

TRADING-VIEW-RED provides a simulated trading environment where users can practice trading without risking real money.

Users can configure virtual capital, execute simulated buy and sell orders, manage open positions, and monitor portfolio performance.

Supported simulated order types can include market, limit, stop-loss, stop-limit, and bracket-style orders.

The virtual portfolio tracks:

- Available virtual balance
- Open positions
- Average entry price
- Realized profit and loss
- Unrealized profit and loss
- Trading history
- Position sizes
- Overall portfolio performance

This feature allows beginners to learn trading fundamentals and experienced traders to test strategies before considering live execution.

## 7. Options Chain and Derivatives Analysis

TRADING-VIEW-RED includes a dedicated workspace for derivatives traders.

The options analysis module is designed to provide:

- Complete options chain
- Call and put prices
- Strike prices
- Open interest
- Change in open interest
- Implied volatility
- Trading volume
- Bid and ask prices
- Put-call ratio
- Expiry selection
- Option Greeks, where available

Users can compare different strike prices, monitor changes in open interest, and analyze derivatives market activity.

## 8. Real-Time Alerts and Notifications

Users can create customizable alerts based on price levels, percentage changes, technical indicators, volume spikes, and strategy conditions.

Examples include:

- NIFTY crossing a specified level.
- RSI moving above 70.
- EMA 9 crossing EMA 21.
- A stock breaking its previous day's high.
- Volume exceeding a configured threshold.

Notifications can be delivered through supported channels such as in-app notifications, Telegram, email, and webhooks.

## 9. AI-Powered Market Analysis

TRADING-VIEW-RED aims to integrate artificial intelligence into the trading workflow.

Its AI assistant can analyze available market information, interpret technical indicators, identify potential chart patterns, and generate structured market reports.

An AI market review can include:

1. Current market direction.
2. Important support and resistance levels.
3. Technical indicator observations.
4. Volume and momentum analysis.
5. Bullish and bearish scenarios.
6. Potential risks and invalidation levels.

The objective is to help users understand market conditions rather than blindly follow automated signals.

AI-generated analysis is informational and does not guarantee profitable trading outcomes.

## 10. Developer-First Open-Source Architecture

TRADING-VIEW-RED is designed for developers who want complete customization and control over their trading environment.

Its architecture should support modular extensions, external market-data integrations, custom indicators, broker APIs, and automated trading strategies.

Developers can:

- Customize the interface.
- Create custom indicators.
- Build their own market scanners.
- Integrate external data providers.
- Develop algorithmic trading strategies.
- Extend the notification system.
- Connect supported broker APIs.
- Self-host the application.
- Contribute directly to the source code.

The platform aims to build a collaborative ecosystem where developers and traders can continuously improve trading technology.

## 11. Security, Privacy and Transparency

TRADING-VIEW-RED prioritizes user control and secure integrations.

The platform should implement secure authentication, encrypted credential storage, protected API endpoints, role-based access controls, and audit logging.

Broker credentials and API tokens must be handled securely and never exposed through public interfaces.

Users should retain control over their accounts, integrations, and personal trading information.

## 12. No Artificial Premium Restrictions

The central philosophy of TRADING-VIEW-RED is that professional trading tools should not be artificially restricted.

Advanced charting, technical indicators, drawing tools, watchlists, market scanners, backtesting, paper trading, alerts, and customization should be accessible without expensive premium subscriptions.

The project is intended to be open-source, transparent, community-driven, and extensible.

Users should be able to inspect the code, contribute improvements, report issues, and develop their own extensions.

## Our Vision

TRADING-VIEW-RED is more than a charting application. It represents an open-source approach to financial technology, where traders have access to powerful analytical tools and developers have the freedom to innovate.

We believe trading technology should empower users, encourage transparency, and eliminate unnecessary restrictions.
